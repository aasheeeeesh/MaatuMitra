# Consent form (English)

**Study:** MaatuMitra class project, reviewer study (E7). Synthetic data only.

- You will check 20 short, made-up conversations in Kannada against a draft note and say
  whether the note has a mistake. It takes about 30 minutes.
- No real patient is involved. All conversations and notes are invented by a computer.
- Some notes come with warning flags. Some flags are deliberately wrong or missing; we will
  explain this at the end.
- We record only a code (like P03), how long each check took, and your answers. We do not
  record your name with your answers.
- You may stop at any time without giving a reason. Stopping has no effect on your grades or
  on anything else.
- Results are reported only as group numbers in a class report.
