## E. Disagreements with .claude/skills/kannada-nlp/negation_table.yaml

1. The table's soft pattern `(?<!ಿ)ಲ್ಲಾ?$` matches ಬೆಲ್ಲ (jaggery) and any ...ೆಲ್ಲ/ುಲ್ಲ word. m4 requires a bare consonant and is safer. m3 uses the table, so m3 over-flags such words (soft).
2. The table has no negative-question cue (ಇಲ್ಲವಾ, ಇಲ್ವಾ), no ಅಲ್ಲದೆ/ಇಲ್ಲದೆ soft cue, no S-VALLA, S-DANTE, S-DIRU, V-AAGADE, V-ISADE. Its verb list has 10 stems (m4: 18, no ಆಗ), so ಆಗದೆ is missed by the table.
3. Clause splitting: the table splits at ANY "." or ",", which cuts ಎಂ.ಜಿ / ಎಂ.ಎಲ್ and decimals; m4 requires whitespace. m4 is better.
4. Both share the blind spot C+ಬೇಡಿ (ತಗೋಬೇಡಿ).
5. Table example NEG-W05 "ಕೊಡುವುದು ಬೇಡಿ" is unnatural Kannada (see m1 review).
