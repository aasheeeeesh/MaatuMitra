## Summary

- AWaRe: all 18 antibiotic classes match the WHO portal (U1). No wrong AWaRe class. Which edition the portal shows (2023 or 2025) is UNVERIFIED.
- Most important problem (CRITICAL): the paediatric templates put ADULT doses into a child's mouth. Paracetamol only renders as 1000 mg (or "two tablets") because of the 1000-1000 range, and ibuprofen renders as 400 mg. These appear in "ಮಗುವಿಗೆ ... ಕೊಡಿ" (give to the child) sentences, with up to 4 doses a day. The data is synthetic, but the gold labels will teach and score this as correct, and m4 C4 will PASS it because it uses an adult range.
- Dose ranges taken from US labels flag normal Indian doses: paracetamol 500/650 mg, ondansetron 4 mg, metformin 1000 mg, ibuprofen 200 mg and metronidazole 200 mg are all NLEM strengths that fall OUTSIDE the stored range.
- Twelve drugs have no range, ORS has no unit at all, and iron-folic acid has no strengths. Under m4's fail-closed policy, every dosed gold mention of these drugs is flagged.
- The ISMP pairs and NLEM rows were not re-verified (the fetches failed). The builder's labels for them stand as they are and stay UNVERIFIED.

## Findings (severity, row/field, suggested value, source)

### CRITICAL
- **M1-C1 Paediatric templates use adult doses** (a3, e8, h2, h3, i5; e7 is fine). For example e8: "ಮಗುವಿಗೆ ವಾಂತಿ ಮತ್ತು ಜ್ವರ" followed by paracetamol `{dose_tab}` (1000 mg tablet, or 2 tablets) `ದಿನಕ್ಕೆ ನಾಲ್ಕು ಬಾರಿ` (four times a day). U3: children are dosed by age/weight with 120 mg/5 ml or 250 mg/5 ml liquid, and 500 mg tablets start only at age 10. U11: "children under 12 years: ask a doctor". Fix: in child-encounter templates use `dose: none`, or add paediatric liquid forms (ml + mg/5 ml strength) together with an age band. Never sample from adult `mg_doses` for a child. Confidence high.

### MAJOR
- **M1-M1 drug-19 paracetamol** `adult_dose_range_mg` 1000-1000 should be **500-1000**, and `max_daily_mg` 3000 should be **4000**. U2: one dose is 1 or 2 x 500 mg, max 8 x 500 mg in 24 h. 3000 is a US OTC consumer limit (U11). 650 mg (NLEM strength) lies inside 500-1000. Side effect today: gold "1000 mg x 4/day" exceeds 3000 and is flagged by C4 although it is a standard adult regimen.
- **M1-M2 drug-24 ondansetron** range 8-24 should be **4-24** (U7: 4 mg doses, tablets 4/8/16/24 mg). The NLEM strength 4 mg is currently out of range, and the generator falls back to 8 mg, which is not the NLEM strength.
- **M1-M3 drug-25 metformin** range 500-850 should be **500-1000** (U6: 1000 mg tablet, max 2550 mg/day in divided doses; U4: 1-3 times a day). The NLEM 1000 mg strength is flagged now. Using 1000 mg as a single dose is a derivation (the label's dosing text names only 500/850 mg as starting doses).
- **M1-M4 drug-20 ibuprofen** range 300-800 should be **200-800** (U10: OTC 200 mg, 1-2 tablets; U8: Rx 400 mg q4-6h, max 3200). Keep `max_daily_mg` 3200 (Rx) but note the OTC max of 1200 (U10).
- **M1-M5 drug-07 metronidazole** range 250-2000 starts above the NLEM 200 mg strength. Suggest **200-2000** (the 200 mg figure is the builder's NLEM extraction, UNVERIFIED by me).
- **M1-M6 drug-08 cotrimoxazole** range 800-800 flags a single 400 mg SMX tablet (NLEM strength). Suggest **400-800** SMX per dose. UNVERIFIED: not re-fetched; the builder's label note says "1 DS or 2 SS tablets q12h".
- **M1-M7 Blank ranges (12 rows)**: drug-02, 10, 17, 18, 21, 22, 29, 30, 31, 32, 33, 34. Every dosed gold mention of these drugs is flagged by m4 C4 (fail-closed). A human should fill each one from a fetched label or the Indian National Formulary. I did NOT fetch values for them, so none are suggested.
- **M1-M8 Units/forms**: drug-21 ORS has no unit. It should be `sachet` (1 sachet in 1 litre) or `ml` of solution, not mg (U5 gives no dose; the exact sachet size is UNVERIFIED). drug-22 zinc is a paediatric 20 mg dispersible tablet for 10-14 days (U5), so an "adult_dose_range_mg" column does not fit it. drug-34 iron-folic acid needs per-component strengths (iron in mg, folic acid in mcg); this is a known mg/mcg confusion risk. Combination rows (drug-02, drug-08) store one component only, so a spoken "625 mg" (amoxicillin 500 + clavulanate 125) cannot be matched. Add total-strength aliases.
- **M1-M9 Ambiguous short forms**: drug-15 `levo` / `ಲೇವೋ` maps to levofloxacin. In everyday Indian speech "levo" often means levocetirizine (not in the lexicon), so this mapping can create a wrong-drug record. Suggest removing `levo`. This is AI clinical judgement, UNVERIFIED. Review `metro`, `dexa`, `diclo` and `amlo` the same way.
- **M1-M10 Template e2** has the patient ask "ಎರಡೂ ಮಾತ್ರೆ ಒಟ್ಟಿಗೆ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳಬಹುದಾ?" (can I take both together?) and the pharmacist answer "ಹೌದು" (yes), for ANY random pair. That pair can be ciprofloxacin or levofloxacin or doxycycline plus zinc or iron-folic acid. U9 says ciprofloxacin must be taken 2 h before or 6 h after zinc/iron. Fix: exclude chelating pairs and same-class pairs (ibuprofen + diclofenac), or drop the "yes".
- **M1-M11 D-62 (stopped early = negated)**: clinically, "took 2 days then stopped" is not the same as "never took". With `negated: true`, a reader may think there was no antibiotic exposure, which matters for treatment failure and resistance. Recommend a record.v2 `adherence` field (taken / partially_taken / not_taken). Needs human.
- **M1-M12 Confusable gaps**: 12 drugs have no confusable (for example doxycycline, nitrofurantoin, paracetamol, ibuprofen, dexamethasone), so E4 falls back to a random drug. Clinically important within-lexicon pairs to add: dexamethasone <-> prednisolone (both steroids, roughly 6x potency difference, so a swap is a dose error) and diclofenac <-> ibuprofen. These are AI-proposed and UNVERIFIED. The builder's ISMP pairs could not be re-verified (PDF unreadable).

### MINOR
- **M1-m1 NLEM membership** UNVERIFIED for drug-09, 16, 21, 26, 27 (cephalexin, erythromycin, ORS, amlodipine, omeprazole). My unverified recollection is that ORS, amlodipine and omeprazole are in NLEM 2022. A human must check the PDF.
- **M1-m2 drug-03 azithromycin / drug-06 doxycycline**: the upper bounds (2000 / 300 mg) are single-dose regimens. With a frequency of 1/day for 3-5 days, these doses would be an overdose that C4 cannot see (no max_daily). Add a max_daily (UNVERIFIED values).
- **M1-m3 drug-10 ceftriaxone** in g7: all strengths 250-2000 mg are used as a 5-day once-daily course. 250 mg/day for 5 days is atypical for an adult (UNVERIFIED).
- **M1-m4 "ಎಲ್ಲ ಮಾತ್ರೆ ಮುಗಿಸಿ" (finish all tablets)** in e1/i2 is attached to as-needed drugs (paracetamol, cetirizine). It is harmless but clinically odd. "Finish the course" is only meaningful for antibiotics.
- **M1-m5 "only if fever" (g5, i5)** is an as-needed instruction, but the record stores a fixed frequency_per_day. The schema cannot express it.
- **M1-m6 AWaRe edition**: U1 does not show a year. A human should confirm against the WHO 2023 (or 2025) AWaRe file.
