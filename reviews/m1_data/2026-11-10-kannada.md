## B. Refreeze templates (rewrite suggestions; child dose as a syrup in ml, or no dose)

Principles: a child's medicine is given by a caregiver, so the verb is ಕೊಡಿ / ಕುಡಿಸಿ, not ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳಿ. Syrup doses are ml (`ಐದು ಎಂ.ಎಲ್`, `5 ml`, or `ಒಂದು ಚಮಚ`). Drug name in Latin or Kannada script both fine. ಸಿರಪ್ is the spoken word (lay: ಟಾನಿಕ್, ಜ್ವರದ ಔಷಧಿ).
