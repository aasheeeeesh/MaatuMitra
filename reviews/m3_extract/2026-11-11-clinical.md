## Summary

The rules pipeline copies the gold convention faithfully, so on DEV it scores 0 critical. That number is real but narrow. It measures only six error classes (negation, drug, dose value/unit, frequency, duration, unsupported). The record format (`record.v1`) has no field for route, liquid strength, patient age/weight, "taken in the past" versus "advised", or "as needed". So three of the ten critical classes in `docs/severity.md` (CR-ROUTE, CR-PAEDIATRIC, CR-LIQUID-STRENGTH) cannot be produced by the scorer at all, and several misleading-but-"correct" records pass. The README is honest about in-sample status but does not say this. Fixing it needs a schema change (record.v2) or an explicit "not measurable" line, not a rules tweak.

## CRITICAL

- **M3-C1 Route is dropped.** `m1-g7-000` turn 0: `ceftriaxone 1000 ಎಂ.ಜಿ injection ದಿನಕ್ಕೆ ಒಂದು ಬಾರಿ ಐದು ದಿವಸ`. Gold and rules output both give `dose 1000 mg, 1/day, 5 days`; the word "injection" is only inside the evidence span, not in a field. A reader sees "ceftriaxone 1000 mg once daily" with no route. Why it matters: ceftriaxone is a powder for injection (NLEM per `drugs.csv`); a record that does not say "injection" can be read as a tablet, and the same for ORS "drink" versus any injection. Fix: add `route` (oral, injection, drops, topical, inhaled) to record.v2 and gold; until then report CR-ROUTE as "not measurable". Confidence high (schema read: `dose_unit` enum has no route).
- **M3-C2 Child syrup volume has no strength and no age.** `m1-e8-000` turn 2: `ಪ್ಯಾರಸಿಟಮಾಲ್ ಸಿರಪ್ 2.5 ml ದಿನಕ್ಕೆ ನಾಲ್ಕು ಸಾರಿ ಮೂರು ದಿನ` -> `2.5 ml, 4/day, 3 days`; `m1-i5-001` turn 1: `ಪ್ಯಾರಸಿಟಮಾಲ್ ಸಿರಪ್ 10 ಎಂ.ಎಲ್` -> `10 ml`. Why it matters: per U1, 10 ml means 240 mg with the 120 mg/5 ml infant liquid (age 4-5) but 500 mg with the 250 mg/5 ml liquid (age 10-11); 2.5 ml is for age 3-5 months only. Without strength or age the number cannot be checked and a prescriber could carry "10 ml" to the wrong bottle. Also, in `m1-i5-001` the caregiver only says "ಮಗು" (child) with no age, so 10 ml is not a safe example to teach. Fix: add `strength` (mg per ml or mg/5 ml) and `patient_age_band` to record.v2; the template should state age or say "ask the doctor". Confidence high on U1; the template concern is mine.
- **M3-C3 Three critical classes cannot be scored; README does not say so.** `m3_extract/scoring.py` maps only CR-NEG, CR-DRUG, CR-DOSE, CR-FREQ, CR-DUR, CR-UNSUPPORTED. `docs/severity.md` (D-91) added CR-ROUTE, CR-PAEDIATRIC, CR-LIQUID-STRENGTH "in reports these count as CRITICAL", but `dev_scores.csv` has no rows for them. "critical 0/2704" in README therefore means "0 of the measurable critical classes". Fix: add one README sentence listing the unmeasured classes, and add the fields in M3-C1/M3-C2 so the classes become scorable. Confidence high.

## MAJOR

- **M3-M1 `negated` means three different things.** `m1-c3-000`: `ಸೆಫ್ಯುರಾಕ್ಸಿಮ್ ಎಲ್ಲ ಮುಗಿಸಿದಿರಾ? / ಇಲ್ಲ.` -> `cefuroxime negated:true` (course NOT finished, so the patient DID take some). Holdout `m1-c5-000..003`: `... ಸೆಫಾಡ್ರಾಕ್ಸಿಲ್ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳಬಹುದಾ? / ಇಲ್ಲ.` -> also `negated:true` (advised not to take). A third meaning is "never taken". A reader will assume `negated:true` = no exposure. This repeats M1-M11 / D-62 (needs human) in the extractor output. Fix: adherence/intent field (taken_complete, taken_partial, not_taken, advised_against). Confidence medium-high. Antibiotic course completion is the clinically weakest point of this module.
- **M3-M2 No "who said it / when" attribute.** `m1-a3-000` turn 1: caregiver says `ನಿನ್ನೆ ರಾತ್ರಿ ಪ್ಯಾರಸಿಟಮಾಲ್ ಸಿರಪ್ ಕೊಟ್ಟೆ` ("I gave paracetamol syrup last night") -> `paracetamol negated:false`, no dose: the same shape as a prescription. `_advice()` and `_med_mentions()` in `rules.py` never read `speaker_role`. Hypothetical (code read, not run): the phrase `ADV-NO-ANTIBIOTIC-COLD` has a positive gloss "take antibiotics for a cold", so a caregiver or patient saying `ನೆಗಡಿಗೆ ಆಂಟಿಬಯಾಟಿಕ್ ಬೇಕು` could become advice with `negated:false`, which is the opposite of stewardship advice. Fix: carry speaker role into the record, and make requests/questions from non-clinicians never produce advice. Confidence medium.
- **M3-M3 "As needed" and food timing are not on the medication.** `m1-i5-001`: `ಜ್ವರ ಇದ್ದರೆ ಮಾತ್ರ ... ಕೊಡಿ` ("give only if fever") -> medication `frequency_per_day:null` plus a separate advice string "only if there is fever". Anyone reading only the medication row sees an unconditional dose. There is no parser for SOS/ಬೇಕಾದಾಗ/before-food (code read; grep found none). `ADV-AFTER-FOOD` is a free-floating advice item, not tied to a drug: in `m1-e4-001` three drugs are dictated and "ಊಟದ ನಂತರ" (after food) is said only after omeprazole, but the record has one global "take after food". Also the template tells omeprazole "after food"; U2 says "before meals" (see M3-m4). Fix: `as_needed` boolean and `food_timing` (before/after/with/none) on each medication; link advice to a drug when in the same clause. Confidence high on the structure, medium on clinical priority.
- **M3-M4 Frequency parser can read "every 2 days" as daily (code read, not run).** `units.py` accepts `ದಿನಕ್ಕೆ <N> ಬಾರಿ` and ignores a number before ದಿನಕ್ಕೆ. For `ಎರಡು ದಿನಕ್ಕೆ ಒಂದು ಬಾರಿ` ("once every two days") the loop meets ದಿನಕ್ಕೆ + ಒಂದು + ಬಾರಿ and sets `frequency_per_day = 1`. Also not supported (so they become null, which is safe but loses data): 1-0-1 style, ಬೆಳಿಗ್ಗೆ/ರಾತ್ರಿ, ಎರಡು ಹೊತ್ತು, every-8-hours, bedtime. `ವಾರಕ್ಕೆ` (per week) is correctly NOT read as per day. Fix: add a regression test where a number sits right before ದಿನಕ್ಕೆ and make the parser return null with a note. Confidence medium (UNVERIFIED: not executed).
- **M3-M5 Strength versus quantity are one field (code read).** `parse_attributes` keeps the FIRST number+unit (`a.dose_value is None` guard). `ibuprofen 200 mg ಮಾತ್ರೆ ದಿನಕ್ಕೆ ಮೂರು ಬಾರಿ` (`m1-h6-000`, holdout) -> `200 mg` (that is the strength). For `500 mg, ಎರಡು ಮಾತ್ರೆ` the record gets 500 mg and the "two tablets" is dropped, so a reader could think 500 mg per dose when it is 1000 mg. For `ಎರಡು ಮಾತ್ರೆ 500 mg` the reverse: `2 tablet` with the strength lost. Fix: separate `strength` and `quantity` fields; if both are present and cannot be combined, return null and flag. Confidence medium.
- **M3-M6 Spoken unit words map to a single unit without a measure (D-93, units.py).** `ಚಮಚ` -> `teaspoon` (the file itself says UNVERIFIED). A Kannada "spoon" may be a 5 ml teaspoon or a 15 ml tablespoon: a threefold syrup error. `ಪ್ಯಾಕೆಟ್` -> `sachet` for every use: "two packets of tablets" would be recorded as 2 sachets. Neither occurs in the dev gold I read. Fix: keep `ಚಮಚ` as null with a note until a speaker confirms; allow `ಪ್ಯಾಕೆಟ್` -> sachet only when the drug is ORS. Confidence medium; model knowledge, low confidence for the size of spoon.
- **M3-M7 Combination products are untested in m3 (code read).** `drugs.csv` gives amoxicillin-clavulanate the roman alias `625` and gives amoxicillin the clipped form `ಅಮಾಕ್ಸಿ`/`amox`. Likely effects: (a) `amox 625` creates two drug mentions (amoxicillin and amoxicillin-clavulanate), and the amoxicillin one is an unsupported drug; (b) "625 mg" is consumed as the drug name so the dose is lost; (c) the digits 625 elsewhere in a transcript would add a drug nobody said (CR-UNSUPPORTED). `grep` of `m3_extract/tests` finds no test for 625 or for iron-folic acid. For IFA (`strengths_mg` blank) a spoken "100 mg" cannot be assigned to iron or to folic acid (mg versus mcg). Fix: add tests for `amoxiclav 625`, `amox 625 mg`, `IFA 1 tablet`; accept `625` only next to a drug stem or an mg unit. Confidence medium; UNVERIFIED (not run).
- **M3-M8 Severity of advice and follow-up errors is under-labelled and not in docs/severity.md.** The scorer emits MA-MISSED-ADVICE, MA-EXTRA-ADVICE, MA-MISSED-FOLLOWUP, MA-EXTRA-FOLLOWUP and MA-WORDING-UNJUDGED as MAJOR; none is in the `docs/severity.md` tables (only rule 5 "unsure -> MAJOR"). On holdout, 40 advice items and 8 follow-ups are missed (`m1-c5-000`: "do not self-medicate; consult a doctor"; `m1-h6-000`: "if symptoms continue, consult a doctor"). These are return-precautions and "do not share antibiotics" messages: omitting them is not purely "information lost". Severity rule 3 says inventing is worse than omitting, but MA-EXTRA-ADVICE equals MA-MISSED-ADVICE. Fix: add the codes to severity.md; treat red-flag follow-up and antibiotic-misuse advice (leftover tablets, antibiotics for a cold) as critical when missed or reversed, and EXTRA-ADVICE that contains a drug or dose as critical. Confidence medium; clinical judgement.
